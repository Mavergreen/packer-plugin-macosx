The Mavericks box can now boot as a CPU with no AVX, with AVX but not
AVX2, or with AVX2. Set `MAVERICKS_CPU_ISA` to `none`, `avx` or `avx2` when
you `vagrant up`; leave it unset and the box boots exactly as before:

```sh
MAVERICKS_CPU_ISA=avx vagrant up --provider qemu
```

- `none` is the default Penryn CPU.
- `avx` is Sandy Bridge: AVX, without AVX2, FMA or BMI.
- `avx2` is Ivy Bridge with Haswell's AVX2, FMA, BMI1 and BMI2. A Haswell
  model hangs 10.9 in a VM, and on a Sandy Bridge model 10.9 never reads the
  CPUID leaf that reports AVX2, so this is the combination that boots and
  reports it.

`avx` and `avx2` need `kvm.ignore_msrs=Y` on the host under KVM, and the
box warns when it is not. They also need a host that has the level's
instructions; a host without them makes QEMU refuse and name what is
missing. Under KVM a level is what the guest is *told*: `none` makes the
AVX family fault, but on a host with AVX2 every other instruction above a
level still runs, and only QEMU's TCG emulator makes them all fault.
`docs/decisions/0009` has the measurements.

The image is unchanged: the levels are chosen at boot, not at install.

The image, and any box made from it, contain Apple's operating system. Never
publish either one.
