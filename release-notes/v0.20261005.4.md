The Mavericks build no longer needs 50 GB of scratch disk to zero the guest's
free space. The target drive now turns the zeros the guest writes into holes
(`detect-zeroes=unmap,discard=unmap`), so the image grows by a few hundred
megabytes during that step instead of to nearly the disk's full size, and the
step is faster. v0.20261005.3's build peaked at 80 GiB of a GitHub runner's
85 GiB free; the box it makes is unchanged.

The image, and any box made from it, contain Apple's operating system. Never
publish either one.
